# AP Recovery Intelligence — Brand System v1

## Core idea
**Evidence Loop**

The brand symbol is built from two directional arcs around a central verification diamond.

- First arc: anomaly / signal enters the audit.
- Diamond: evidence is challenged and verified.
- Second arc: only validated value can progress.
- Open loop: the system is intentionally not a closed black box.

## Brand line
**Evidence before claims. Verification before recovery.**

## Product line
**Find the signal. Prove the case.**

## Visual language
- Deep Ink — #07111F
- Electric Blue — #315CFF
- Signal Cyan — #18CAD2
- Verification Violet — #8E63FF
- Evidence Brass — #D0A757
- Warm Porcelain — #F7F4EE

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk
- Interface/body: Manrope
- Evidence/data: IBM Plex Mono

## Design rules
1. Never use color as the only carrier of meaning.
2. Never use fabricated client logos or social proof.
3. Keep identified / confirmed / recovered states visually distinct.
4. Use gradients as signal/navigation accents, not decorative noise.
5. Dark sections communicate system/evidence architecture; light sections communicate explanation and trust.
6. Motion must be subtle and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
7. The Evidence Loop mark must retain clear space of at least 25% of its width.

## Voice
Precise, calm, evidence-first, candid about uncertainty. Avoid hype, urgency tricks and unsupported savings language.
