Phase-1 boundary

Transaction evidence first. Statement evidence when authorized.

A limited export can show unusual relationships, but absence of a ledger reference is never treated as proof by itself.

RULE / 04No vendor contact before client verification.
01
Duplicate & overpayment analysis

Evaluate transaction relationships from an authorized AP export.

02
Credit confirmation

May require the vendor ledger, subsequent periods or a supplier statement.

03
No automatic outreach

Supplier contact requires explicit client approval.

04
No “open credit” claim from absence alone

Limited exports may omit where the credit was applied.
